How to play

Search the locations, use the camera footage, magnifier and fingerprint tools, and question the suspects. Every clue goes into your case notebook. On the accusation board choose who did it, how and why, then pin the three pieces of evidence that prove it. Stuck? Tap the lightbulb to ask Esra for a hint.

Purchases

If you bought the Detective Pass on another device or reinstalled the game, open Settings → Restore purchases. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Ad privacy

Change your ad consent anytime in Settings → Ad Privacy Choices (where available), or disable tracking in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
